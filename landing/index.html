Take the break
your eyes deserve.

Enso Retreat is a small app that periodically blocks your screen and gives you a moment to look away, stretch and rest your eyes — then quietly gets out of your way again.

Download for Windows

Free and open source. Runs quietly until it's time for a break.

How a break works

Locked, but never hidden.

When a break starts, Enso Retreat blocks the keyboard and mouse, so there is nothing left to click or type. A translucent overlay covers the desktop, and everything behind it stays in plain sight.

A heads-up first

An alert appears shortly before the break, and you can delay it if you're in the middle of something.

Hands off

Keystrokes and clicks don't reach your apps until the break is over. Nothing to do but look away and rest.

Still in the loop

Watch a download finish or a build run through the overlay. The numpad + and − keys make it more or less see-through.

Why it helps

Small nudges, on your terms.

A handful of settings, none of them mandatory.

Rest your eyes

Let yourself rest, look afar and run through a quick eye-gymnastics routine on every break.

Set your own rhythm

Configure the timing however you wish — a classic Pomodoro schedule, or anything else, down to a custom Crontab pattern.

Stays out of your way

Breaks can be skipped automatically while a chosen app is running, or when there's been no activity to interrupt.

Make it yours

Any picture can be shown as a gentle, transparent overlay while the screen is locked — your favourite wallpapers included.

Quotes

A thought to rest on.

Turn on quotes, and every break brings a short line of wisdom on a dark card in the middle of the screen. Fifty come built in, from Marcus Aurelius and Seneca to Lao Tzu, and you can replace them with your own.

Your own words

One quote per line, the author after a dash. Shown at random or in order, never the same one twice in a row.

A picture above

Rodin's Thinker, any image of your own, or no picture at all, just the quote.

Set in your type

Any installed TrueType font, in italic or upright, in the colour and size you like.

Stars or plain dark

Keep your break screen around the card, or swap it for a calm, dimmed backdrop.

See it in action

A minute of your day, back.

Enso Retreat is already embedded into Enso Launcher as an optional module, but here you can download it as a standalone app.
